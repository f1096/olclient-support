隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日。适用于 OlClient。

我们如何处理信息

OlClient 是连接你自行选择的 OpenList 服务的客户端。服务地址、用户名、连接别名及应用偏好保存在设备上；会话令牌存储在设备 Keychain。登录密码和两步验证码仅用于本次认证，不作为保存连接资料持久化。

登录请求、文件路径及你发起的文件和管理操作发送至你配置的服务。下载、媒体与缩略图可能从该服务返回的存储提供方地址读取。服务运营者和存储提供方各自的隐私政策适用于他们收到的信息。OlClient 的发行者不提供用于接收你的网盘文件、密码或服务令牌的文件托管后台。

本机文件和缓存

预览会使用临时副本，图片与专辑封面可能缓存于本机。本机下载会保留，直到你删除它们。只有在你选择后，文件才会导出到系统“文件”、照片图库或其他分享目标；导出的副本由目标应用及系统管理。你可以从设置清除封面缓存，并在下载管理中删除本机副本。删除本机副本不会自动删除远端原文件。

设备权限与身份验证

照片选择使用系统选择器；将兼容图片或视频保存到照片图库时请求添加权限。访问系统文件目录使用你明确选择的目录授权。离线文件入口可使用 Face ID 或 Touch ID 验证设备身份；App 只接收系统验证结果，无法访问面部或指纹模板。OlClient 没有额外的应用启动锁。

广告、分析与第三方组件

OlClient 的应用源码未接入广告、行为分析或跨应用跟踪 SDK。内置媒体组件在设备上解码，按你请求读取本地文件或配置服务返回的网络媒体。Apple 系统诊断和 App Store 服务受 Apple 的政策管理。

你的选择

你可以移除保存的连接、退出服务、清除缓存或删除本机下载。服务端账号、远端文件以及服务日志请联系你所使用的服务运营者处理。移除 App 不会删除远端账号或文件；如需撤销保存在设备上的登录，请先退出服务，或在服务端撤销会话。

联系与更新

Information handling

OlClient connects to an OpenList service chosen by you. Server addresses, usernames, aliases and preferences are stored on your device. Session tokens are stored in the device Keychain. Passwords and two-step codes are used for authentication and are not persisted as saved connection data.

Authentication requests, file paths and operations you initiate are sent to your configured service. Downloads, media and thumbnails may be read from storage-provider URLs returned by that service. Their operators apply their own privacy policies. The OlClient publisher does not operate a file-hosting backend to receive your cloud files, passwords or service tokens.

Files and caches

Previews can use temporary copies. Images and album artwork can be cached locally. Downloads remain until deleted. Exports to Files, Photos or other apps happen when requested; exported copies are managed by their destination. Clear artwork caches in Settings and remove local downloads in download management. Removing a local copy does not delete its remote original.

Permissions and device authentication

Photo imports use the system picker; saving compatible images or videos requests add-only Photos access. File-directory access uses the directory you select. Offline files may require Face ID or Touch ID. The app receives only the system authentication result and cannot access biometric templates. There is no additional in-app launch lock.

Advertising, analytics and components

The app source includes no advertising, behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking SDK. Built-in media components decode on the device and read local or service-provided media at your request. Apple diagnostics and App Store services are governed by Apple's policies.

Your choices and contact

Remove saved connections, sign out, clear caches or delete local downloads in the app. Contact your server operator about server accounts, remote files and server logs. Removing the app does not delete remote accounts or files. To revoke a saved login, sign out first or revoke the session on your server.

Contact fat.fox.1096@icloud.com about privacy. If you email support, your message and attachments are received to answer and investigate your request. Do not send passwords, tokens or private cloud content. Changes will be dated on this page.
